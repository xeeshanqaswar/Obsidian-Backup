
## 2.1. Setting and Lore

- The world is set on a **mystical floating island continent** orbiting a glowing moon.
- The land is steeped in tradition, with **seasonal festivals** tied to celestial cycles.
- Magic exists subtly, manifesting in **enchanted crops** and **rare creatures**.
- Villagers maintain **deep-rooted cultures** and **unique professions**, each biome influenced by generations of lore.
- The world operates on a **12-month calendar** with **4 seasons**, each 28 in-game days long.

## 2.2. Story Premise

The player inherits a neglected farm on the edge of a bustling village. As they rebuild the farm, they uncover ancient secrets linking the land's prosperity to a mystical energy from the moon. Through farming, forging friendships, and exploration, the player becomes a key figure in restoring balance to the world.

## 2.3. Story Progression & Chapters

1.  **Chapter 1: Arrival and Foundation**
    - Player moves to the village, restores basic farm.
2.  **Chapter 2: Growing Roots**
    - Introductions to villagers, unlocks first festivals and farm upgrades.
3.  **Chapter 3: The Whispering Woods**
    - Explore magical forest biome, find mythical seeds.
4.  **Chapter 4: Beneath the Mines**
    - Mining introduces ancient ruins and lore.
5.  **Chapter 5: The Celestial Festival**
    - The moon's power becomes central, unlocking rare crops and mystical animals.
6.  **Chapter 6: Balance Restored**
    - Final story arc with major town expansion, permanent upgrades, and post-game sandbox mode.

## 2.4. Themes and Emotional Tone

- Cozy and heartwarming, yet with a sense of wonder and mystery.
- Focus on growth, relationships, and balance with nature.
- Emotional arcs: from solitude to belonging, from neglect to prosperity.

## 2.5. Player's Role in the World

- Newcomer farmer restoring a family legacy.
- Catalyst for positive change, helping townsfolk and solving mysteries.
- Farmer, craftsman, and adventurer balancing rural life and exploration.

## 2.6. Key Locations & Biomes

1.  **Village Center** -- Shops, community buildings, festivals.
2.  **Player's Farm** -- Fully customizable hub.
3.  **Enchanted Forest** -- Magical plants, rare animals, hidden ruins.
4.  **Mines and Caverns** -- Ore tiers, underground lakes, treasure chambers.
5.  **Mountain Peaks** -- Seasonal hiking trails, rare herbs.
6.  **Coastal Bay** -- Fishing, beach festivals, aquatic resources.
7.  **Mystic Glade** -- Unlockable endgame biome tied to story.
