
## 7.1. Narrative Theme Overview

- **Core Themes:** Community, growth, cycles of nature, mystery of the moon's magic.
- **Tone:** Warm, grounded farming life with light magical realism and soft fantasy elements.
- **Structure:**
  - Main story unfolds over **two in-game years**.
  - Player inherits a neglected farm and slowly rebuilds the island's prosperity.
  - Magic (moonlight, ancient ruins, spirits) ties into deeper exploration and late-game content.

## 7.2. Narrative Pillars

1. **Player-Driven Progression:** Story reveals as the player farms, explores, and interacts with villagers---no hard deadlines.
2. **Village Bonds:** Each NPC has a personal arc that deepens through gifts, dialogue, and quests.
3. **Seasonal Storytelling:** Festivals and environmental changes reflect narrative growth.
4. **Legacy Mystery:** Ancient ruins and moon shrines hide the island's backstory.

## 7.3. Main Story Outline

| **Act**   | **Key Events & Goals** |
| --------- | ---------------------- |
| **Act I** | Player inherits farm; introduced to village; fixes overgrown land; first shrine discovery. |
| **Act II** | Village revitalization efforts; exploration opens mines, forests; uncover moonstone magic. |
| **Act III** | Ancient ruins reveal island's magical history; main antagonist is not a villain but a spirit bound to the moon's cycle. |
| **Act IV** | Final Moon Festival questline unites villagers; balance restored; sandbox endgame unlocks. |

## 7.8. Relationship System

- **Hearts System:**
  - Max **10 hearts** per NPC.
  - Hearts unlocked via dialogue, gifts, completing their quests, and attending festivals.

| **Heart Level** | **Unlocks** |
| --------------- | ----------- |
| 1-2             | Casual conversation variations. |
| 3-4             | Side tasks, minor items. |
| 5-6             | Personal questlines, small cutscenes. |
| 7-8             | Deeper lore, rare recipes. |
| 9-10            | Marriage/roommate option (romance NPCs). |

## 7.9. Romance System

- **Marriage Candidates:** 8 (Elara, Corvin, Miri, Tessa, Lila, Elias, Nyra, ??? secret character).
- Marriage unlocks **house expansion** and **daily spouse interactions** (helping farm tasks, giving gifts).
- Player can have **1 child or adopt pets** post-marriage.

## 7.10. Dialogue System Spec

- **Dynamic Dialogue Tree:**
  - **Daily Variants:** Greetings change based on time/weather.
  - **Heart Tier Variants:** Unlock deeper personality over time.
  - **Event-Specific Lines:** Festivals, birthdays, and narrative milestones.

Dialogue is stored in **JSON** with keys:

 `{  
 "npc_id": "elara",  
 "heart_level": 3,  
 "weather": "rain",  
 "dialogue": "Stay dry out there! Perfect day for reading."  
 }`
