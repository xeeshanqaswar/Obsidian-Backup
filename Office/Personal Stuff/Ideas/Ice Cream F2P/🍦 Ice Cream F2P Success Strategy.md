
## 🎯 Objective

Build a lean F2P ice cream shop game capable of generating
**\$3,000/month steady income** with 800--1000 DAU.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# ✅ Can It Be Built in 2--3 Months?

Yes --- IF you build the retention engine, not the full content-heavy
game.

## 3-Month Plan Overview

### Month 1 -- Core Loop

-   Order system
-   Ice cream assembly interaction
-   Basic scoring
-   6 flavors
-   3 toppings
-   2 containers

### Month 2 -- Retention Layer

-   Upgrade system
-   Daily login reward
-   Featured flavor rotation
-   Rewarded ads integration
-   First IAP pack
-   10--15 total ingredients

### Month 3 -- Polish & Soft Launch

-   UX refinement
-   Economy tuning
-   Analytics integration
-   App Store Optimization
-   Retention testing

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🔑 Realistic Success Checklist

## 1️⃣ Fast First Unlock

-   New flavor unlocked within 5--10 minutes.
-   Visible progress immediately.

## 2️⃣ Daily Hook System

Must include: - Daily login reward - Daily featured flavor (2x coins) -
Daily challenge - Weekend multiplier

Without daily hooks, DAU collapses.

## 3️⃣ Session Length Target

-   5--8 minutes per session
-   6--10 customers per day
-   Clear "Day Complete" summary screen

## 4️⃣ Smart Ads Strategy

Foundation for \$3K goal:

-   Rewarded ad for double tips
-   Rewarded ad for rare flavor trial
-   Optional ad after session

Avoid forced interstitial spam.

Target: 1--2 voluntary ads per session.

## 5️⃣ Economy Without Friction

-   Unlock something every 1--2 sessions early game
-   Visible upgrade impact
-   No coin starvation
-   Avoid grind walls

If players feel stuck → uninstall.

## 6️⃣ Visual Satisfaction (Critical)

Retention depends on: - Clean scoop animation - Satisfying sauce
drizzle - Sprinkle physics - Juicy coin pop feedback

Polish \> Depth.

## 7️⃣ Clear Long-Term Goal

Players must see progression ladder: - Unlock premium glass bowl - Reach
Shop Level milestones - Expand shop visually

No visible ladder = no long-term engagement.

## 8️⃣ Analytics From Day 1

Track: - D1 retention - D3 retention - D7 retention - Session length -
Ads per session - Coins per session - Drop-off points

Without analytics, you are guessing.

## 9️⃣ Soft Launch Requirement

-   200--500 real users
-   2--3 weeks testing
-   Tune economy
-   Fix retention drop-offs

Do not scale until D1 ≥ 40%.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 📊 Metrics Needed for \$3K/Month

### Scenario: Ads + Light IAP

Target: - 800--1000 DAU - ARPDAU \$0.15--\$0.20 - D1 ≥ 40% - D7 ≥ 15%

At 1000 DAU × \$0.15 ARPDAU: ≈ \$150/day → ≈ \$4,500/month (before
platform cuts).

Even at 800 DAU, goal is achievable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# ❌ Avoid in First 3 Months

-   Multiplayer
-   Large story mode
-   Deep decor editor
-   Massive flavor library
-   Complex systems

Focus on polish and retention.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🎯 Strategic Focus Order

Retention \> Satisfaction \> Stability \> Scale

Not: Content Depth \> Feature Count \> Complexity

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 📅 Realistic Timeline to \$3K/Month

-   Month 1--3: Build MVP
-   Month 4--6: Iterate & optimize retention
-   Month 6--9: Stabilize DAU and monetization

Steady income requires iteration, not luck.
