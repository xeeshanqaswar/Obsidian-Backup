
## 🔹 Design Goals

- **Clear Early, Mid, and Late Game Flow:** Prevents content overload at the start while ensuring long-term depth.
- **Skill-Based, Not Just XP:** Players improve through active farming, fishing, mining, combat, and crafting.
- **Tool and Mechanic Unlocks:** Players earn progression through exploration, quests, and mastery milestones.
- **Player Customization:** Stat growth and perks allow unique playstyles.
- **Content Pacing:** Each in-game year introduces **new biomes, festivals, and systems** to maintain engagement.

## 🔹 Player Core Stats

| **Stat**        | **Description**                 | **Effect on Gameplay** |
| --------------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| **Stamina**     | Energy pool for all actions.    | Increases with level; reduces early-game grind. |
| **Health**      | Used in combat or hazards (lava, brambles, etc.). | Gated combat exploration areas. |
| **Strength**    | Impacts melee damage and mining break speed. | Faster resource gathering, more damage. |
| **Dexterity**   | Influences fishing minigame speed, dodge roll speed. | Advanced mobility/fishing perks. |
| **Luck**        | Affects rare drops, shiny crops, crit chances. | Scales late-game resource farming. |
| **Charisma**    | NPC relationship boost, merchant discounts. | Encourages social quest line progression. |

## 🔹 XP & Level System Overview

| **Level Band**   | **Features Unlocked**       | **Description** |
| ---------------- | --------------------------- | --------------- |
| **Lv. 1–5 (Starter)** | Basic tools, farm plots, coop animals, Spring crops. | Tutorial, intro NPC quests, farm setup. |
| **Lv. 6–10 (Novice)** | Barn, fishing rod upgrades, cooking basics. | Unlock Meadowlands, beginner combat. |
| **Lv. 11–20 (Apprentice)** | Greenhouse quest, forest shrine access, tool Lv2. | Deeper farming cycles, rare seeds. |
| **Lv. 21–30 (Journeyman)** | Cavern access, mine carts, artisan machines. | First magical crops, mid-tier combat zones. |
| **Lv. 31–40 (Expert)** | Mount stables, Frostpeak unlock, magical tools. | Unlocks weather control gear, stamina boost food. |
| **Lv. 41–50 (Master)** | Ruins access, legendary crops, shrine rituals. | Endgame economy, pets with travel perks. |
| **Lv. 51+ (Legend)** | Prestige system, rare pet genetics, ultimate gear crafting. | Infinite scaling content, festivals expand. |

## 🔹 Skill Tracks

Each skill has its own XP bar and levels separately. Each skill level unlocks recipes, perks, and specialized gear.

| **Skill Name** | **Level Effects (Example Progression)** |

| **Farming**    | Lv.1: Plant seeds. Lv.5: Fertilizer. Lv.10: Multi-harvest crops. Lv.15+: Magic Fields. |
| **Mining**     | Lv.1: Stone nodes. Lv.5: Ore nodes. Lv.10: Rare ore. Lv.20: Crystal scanning. |
| **Fishing**    | Lv.1: Pond fish. Lv.5: River fish. Lv.10: Lake fish. Lv.15+: Legendary fishing gear. |

## 🔹 Quest-Based Progression Gate System

| **Chapter / Year** | **Unlocks** |
| ------------------ | ----------- |
| **Year 1 Spring**  | Basic farming, coop, fishing rod, starter festival. |
| **Year 1 Summer**  | Forest biome, beginner combat dungeon, mount tutorial. |
| **Year 1 Fall**    | Caverns access, artisan machines. |
| **Year 1 Winter**  | Greenhouse unlock quest, first shrine ritual. |
| **Year 2 Spring**  | Frostpeak biome, legendary crops. |
| **Year 2+**        | Moonlit Ruins, endgame pets, shrine mastery. |

## 🔹 Prestige & Infinite Scaling (Late Game)

- After Level 50, players enter **Prestige Farming Guilds**:
  - Unlock **cosmetic farm expansions** (build-your-own farm tiles).
  - Introduce **weekly rotating shrine challenges** for rare crafting recipes.
  - Cosmetic-only pet skins, weapon glows, and house themes.
- Ensures **infinite engagement** without power creep breaking balance.
