### Coins

**Source :** Missions, Daily rewards, via ads
**Sink :** Buy most clothing

# **Event Economy**

This is probably where Glow earns the most money.

Events introduce:

- exclusive outfits
- themed collections
- Zodiac items
- Fashion Week collections
- special hairstyles
- collaborations

Each event has its own temporary progression and often uses dedicated event currencies or mechanics.  

Limited availability creates strong urgency.
