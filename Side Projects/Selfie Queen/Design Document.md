## Voting  System

==**the rewards you earn by casting votes** for others, and **the prizes you unlock based on the votes your own outfits receive**==. [[1](https://suitsmeclub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13267578144145-How-It-Works), [2](https://suitsmeclub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13229040794257-Voting)]

---

1. Rewards Earned From Casting Votes (Judging Others)

When you go to the voting section to judge other players' outfits, you receive direct bonuses for your time: [[1](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.style.fashion.game.suitsme), [2](https://suitsmeclub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13229040794257-Voting)]

- **Instant Currencies:** You earn instant **coins and keys/tickets** just for casting a set number of votes.
- **Double Rewards:** If you have the [Premium Access Membership](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C6_Dk_jsfGp/), your active voting rewards are permanently doubled. [[1](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/suitsme-dress-up-fashion-game/id1536929374?see-all=reviews), [2](https://suitsmeclub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13267578144145-How-It-Works), [3](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C6_Dk_jsfGp/)]

---

2. Rewards Earned From Receiving Votes (Your Challenge Submissions)

When a challenge ends, the community votes on your look by comparing it against another player's outfit in head-to-head pairs. [[1](https://suitsmeclub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13267578144145-How-It-Works)]

- **The Star Rating (2.7 to 5.0 Stars):** Every time a real player selects your outfit, your look earns stars. The final score is tallied on a scale ranging from a baseline minimum of **2.7 stars up to a perfect 5.0 stars**. []
- **Tiered Prize Thresholds:** Reaching specific star milestones unlocks increasingly valuable prize tiers:
    
    - **Low/Baseline Stars (2.7 - 3.5):** Generates a small participation payout of basic coins.
    - **Mid Tiers (3.5 - 4.5):** Unlocks larger sums of coins and exclusive clothing items/accessories.
    - **Top Tiers (4.5 - 5.0):** Yields the highest currency payouts, rare premium items, and valuable **Gems**. [[1](https://suitsmeclub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13267578144145-How-It-Works)]
    
- **Season Leaderboard Boosting:** The total stars you accumulate from community votes feed directly into your weekly **Season Leaderboard** standing. Finishing in the top 10 positions on the leaderboard rewards you with massive, exclusive end-of-season item bundles.

## Tournaments & Scoring

**My Understanding**

`let me rephrase there are special challanges called dresscode challanges, and it requires specific glamour points, glamour point is something that is tied to your clothing and when you use them it consumes their glamour point and if you want to use the clothes that loses its glamour points than your can renew their glamour point by using coins`

**Actual System**

**Yes, that is exactly correct!** You have perfectly described how the game's core mechanic operates.

The Glamour Points Cycle Break Down

1. **The Requirements:** Most challenges have a strict dress code that sets a **minimum Glamour Point threshold**. [[1](https://suitsmeclub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13267578144145-How-It-Works)]
2. **The Connection:** Glamour Points are tied directly to individual pieces of clothing. [[1](https://suitsmeclub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13228570156433-Glamour-Points-Requirements)]
3. **The Consumption:** Only **unworn (new)** or **recently renewed** items carry active Glamour Points. As soon as you use an item to submit an outfit, its Glamour Points are depleted for subsequent dress-code challenges. [, [2](https://suitsmeclub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13228570156433-Glamour-Points-Requirements)]
4. **The Renewal (Glamming Up):** If you want to use that same item again and have it count toward the threshold, you must **spend coins to "Glam Up" or renew it**. This resets its Glamour Points back to active status. [, [2](https://suitsmeclub.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13228872107409-Glamming-up-Owned-Items)]
