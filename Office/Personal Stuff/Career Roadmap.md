## 1️⃣ Stay on Unity as Core Skill

- **Reason:** 8+ years of experience is your moat.
- **Strategy:** Don’t abandon Unity; instead, expand into high-value areas like simulations, XR, and enterprise projects.
- **Key Focus Areas:**
  - Clean architecture (SOLID, DI, VContainer)
  - Performance optimization
  - Async workflows, Addressables, Subscenes
  - Enterprise-ready systems
- **Multiplayer/networking** (Photon, Netcode for GameObjects) — high demand, most Unity devs avoid it
- **AR Foundation / ARKit / ARCore** — mobile AR is growing, relatively few specialists
- **SDK integrations** — AdMob, Firebase, analytics, IAP. Every mobile game client needs this and many developers find it tedious
- **Performance optimization** — mobile-specific, directly relevant to your 8 years experience
- **WebGL builds** — Unity games that run in browser, growing demand for interactive web experiences
