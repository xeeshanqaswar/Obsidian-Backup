## Currency Flow
- **Coins Inflow** → Orders (5–50 avg)  
- **Coins Outflow** → Expansions, décor  
- **Gems Inflow** → Level-ups, NPC rewards, events  
- **Gems Outflow** → Premium décor, exclusive recipes
