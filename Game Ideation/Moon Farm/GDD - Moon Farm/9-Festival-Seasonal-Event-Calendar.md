
## 9.1. Seasonal Overview

| **Season** | **Visual Palette & Vibe**         | **Gameplay Focus** |
| ---------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| **Spring** | Soft greens, blooming flowers, light rain showers. | Introduction, farming start, animal raising. |
| **Summer** | Vibrant colors, long sunny days, beach life. | Fishing, foraging, community events. |
| **Autumn** | Warm reds, oranges, golden sunsets. | Harvest, crafting, economy growth. |
| **Winter** | Snow, auroras, festive lights, cozy indoors. | Story progression, rare quests, mining. |

## 9.2. Festival Structure

| **Festival Tier**  | **Description** |
| ------------------ | --------------- |
| **Major Festivals (8)** | Anchor events per season, full cutscenes, unique activities, rewards. |
| **Minor Events (4)** | Half-day activities, skill competitions, special shop unlocks. |
| **Traveling Events (2)** | Rare merchants, exotic pets, seasonal gear. |

- **Festival Day Flow:**
  - Town is decorated (dynamic assets per season).
  - Shops close early; NPCs gather in central plaza or festival area.
  - Player can **participate in minigames** or **unlock rare items**.
  - Ends with **cutscene or fireworks**; next day resets to normal.

## 9.4. Major Festival Details

#### 🌸 Blossom Day Festival (Spring 5)

- **Theme:** Fresh beginnings, bonding with villagers.
- **Decor:** Floral arches, pastel banners, spring lanterns.
- **Gameplay:**
  - Flower hunt mini-game (collect hidden blossoms in town).
  - Dance circle with NPCs; heart point boost.
- **Rewards:**
  - Seasonal flower seeds.
  - Spring-exclusive clothing.

#### 🎣 Fishing Frenzy Derby (Spring 15)

- **Theme:** Community fishing tournament.
- **Decor:** Dock decorations, nets, floating buoys.
- **Gameplay:**
  - Timed fishing competition; leaderboards with prizes.
  - Rare bait vendor appears.
- **Rewards:**
  - Fishing rod upgrade schematic.
  - Unique aquarium décor.

#### 🌙 Moonlight Market (Spring 28)

- **Theme:** Rare traveling merchant pop-up.
- **Decor:** Moonlit stalls, glowing lanterns.
- **Gameplay:**
  - Buy rare seeds, breeding items, exotic recipes.
  - NPCs have unique dialogue hinting at summer events.
- **Rewards:**
  - Summer starter pack (fertilizer, seeds).

#### 🌊 Ocean Spirit Festival (Summer 7)

- **Theme:** Spiritual ritual honoring ocean spirits.
- **Decor:** Ocean platforms, tiki torches, floating lanterns.
- **Gameplay:**
  - Raft race mini-game.
  - Lantern release cutscene with NPC wishes.
- **Rewards:**
  - Rare fishing lure.
  - Cosmetic boat upgrade.

#### 🌽 Crop Fair (Summer 21)

- **Theme:** Competition between farmers.
- **Decor:** Banners with crop icons, harvest baskets.
- **Gameplay:**
  - Player submits their best crop for judging.
  - Random NPC judges with dialogue commentary.
- **Rewards:**
  - Fertilizer upgrade recipes.
  - Golden trophy for display in home.

#### 🍁 Harvest Jubilee (Autumn 10)

- **Theme:** Feast celebrating community harvest.
- **Decor:** Long banquet tables, warm lighting.
- **Gameplay:**
  - Cooking minigame where players use their crops.
  - Winning dish becomes seasonal tavern special.
- **Rewards:**
  - Artisan tools (cheese press, preserves barrel).
  - +1 permanent friendship with all villagers.

#### 🛠 Artisan Day (Autumn 20)

- **Theme:** Craftsmanship showcase.
- **Decor:** Tool stands, market stalls, art displays.
- **Gameplay:**
  - Player submits crafted goods; NPCs react.
  - Blueprint unlock vendor.
- **Rewards:**
  - Advanced crafting stations.

#### ❄️ Snow Lantern Festival (Winter 5)

- **Theme:** Cozy winter mystery event.
- **Decor:** Glowing snow lanterns, snowy village overlay.
- **Gameplay:**
  - Puzzle scavenger hunt with hidden clues.
  - Ice sculpture mini-game.
- **Rewards:**
  - Rare pet accessory.
  - Winter-exclusive décor.

#### 🌙 Moon's Embrace Gala (Winter 25)

- **Theme:** Main story finale celebration.
- **Decor:** Entire town glows with auroras, massive moon backdrop.
- **Gameplay:**
  - Story cutscene triggered (Moon Spirit).
  - Players exchange gifts with villagers.
  - Fireworks display.
- **Rewards:**
  - Endgame unlocks (fast travel, legendary seeds).

- **Art Team:**
  - Dynamic town map overlays per festival (banners, tables, lanterns).
  - NPC seasonal outfits (each festival adds variation).
- **Programming:**
  - Temporary map tiles and NPC schedules.
  - Cutscene triggers post-activity.
  - Minigames built modularly (scavenger hunt, fishing derby).
- **Audio:**
  - Unique seasonal tracks for each festival.
  - Firework sound effects, crowd chatter.
