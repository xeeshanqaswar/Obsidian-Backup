# 🎨 Art Reference Bible

## 🎯 Goal
The bakery should feel **cozy, pastel, inviting**, with strong ASMR appeal. Visuals should relax and satisfy the player.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette
- Pastels → soft pink, cream, mint, lavender  
- Warm neutrals → light wood, beige, soft browns  
- Accent colors → gold, strawberry red, pistachio green  

---

## 📌 Moodboard Sources (for Artist to Study)
- **Bakery Interiors**
  - [Pinterest Board: Cozy Cafés](https://www.pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=cozy%20cafe)
  - Japanese kissaten interiors (small, warm, wood-heavy)
  - European patisseries (bright glass cases, pastries as centerpieces)

- **Food**
  - Lemon Cake game screenshots
  - Cooking Mama series
  - Real pastry photography (croissants, macarons, cupcakes)

- **Characters**
  - Chibi or soft-anime inspired  
  - Round features, cozy clothing (sweaters, aprons)
  - Optional anthropomorphic (cat café style)

- **UI**
  - Rounded buttons, soft pastel gradients
  - Minimal clutter, focus on recipes & décor
  - Example: [Animal Crossing Pocket Camp UI](https://www.pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=pocket%20camp%20ui)

---

## 📑 Deliverable Examples
- **Bakery Room:** pastel walls + wood floor
- **Food Icons:** glossy, hand-painted, ASMR shine
- **Décor:** fairy lights, potted plants, cozy chairs
- **UI Icons:** cupcakes, hearts, stars, coins, gems
