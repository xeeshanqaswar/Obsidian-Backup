

Forget Sensor Tower for now. It's built for publishers with budgets. Here's your actual toolkit:

**For Mobile:**

- **data.ai free tier** — look at "rising" charts, not top grossing
- Search Reddit: **r/androidgaming**, **r/iosgaming** — filter by "hidden gem" posts. These are games with no UA budget that people found organically. Study them obsessively.
- Look for games **ranked #200-500 in their category** consistently for 2+ years. That's sustainable small revenue. #1 is a lottery ticket.

**For Steam (highly recommend you seriously consider this):**

- **Gamalytic.com** — free, shows estimated revenue per game. Filter: "indie", under $10 price, released 2022-2024, 200-1000 reviews. These are your real competitors and real benchmarks.
- **itch.io** — not for revenue, but for validating game concepts cheaply and building an early audience
