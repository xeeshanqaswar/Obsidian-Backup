# 🥐 Baking System & Recipes

## Baking Steps
1. Add Ingredients (drag)
2. Mix (swipe in circle)
3. Knead (tap & hold squish)
4. Bake (wait & tap "ding")
5. Decorate (swipe/tap toppings)
6. Serve (drag to customer)
