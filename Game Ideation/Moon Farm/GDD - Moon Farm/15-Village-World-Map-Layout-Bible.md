
## 🔹 World Design Philosophy

- **Compact but Dense:** Avoid empty areas; every tile has meaning.
- **Vertical Progression:** Areas gated by tools, story quests, or shrine magic.
- **Smooth Travel Flow:** No "dead-end" maps; natural loops encourage exploration.
- **Thematic Biomes:** Each biome has unique flora, fauna, weather, and music.
- **Landmarks as Navigation:** Lighthouses, statues, bridges serve as orientation anchors.

## 🔹 High-Level World Structure

The island is divided into **7 Key Regions** arranged in a circular flow, with the **Village Hub at the center**.

| **Region**        | **Theme & Features**          | **Player Progression Role** |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Central Village** | Player home, NPC homes, core shops. | Game hub, all major facilities accessible. |
| **Farmstead Zone** | Expandable farmland, greenhouse, barns, coops. | Farming & crafting focus. |
| **Meadowlands**   | Forests, wildflowers, streams, beginner forage. | Early exploration, first shrine. |
| **Whisper Cavern** | Caves, mines, crystal chambers, bats/slimes. | Mid-game mining, first combat dungeon. |
| **Frostpeak Highlands** | Snowy biome, icy fishing, mountain shrine. | Mid/late-game zone, rare animals & ores. |
| **Sunset Shores** | Beaches, lighthouse, fishing village. | Fishing hub, exotic merchant trade. |
| **Moonlit Ruins** | Endgame ruins, magical biome, final shrine. | Endgame exploration, prestige content. |

## 🔹 Central Village Layout (Exact Placements)

| **Location**      | **Placement Detail**          | **Purpose** |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------- |
| **Town Square**   | Center hub, near bulletin board & fountain. | NPC gatherings, festivals. |
| **Player Farmhouse** | South of village, easy farm-to-village travel. | Player base. |

| **Blacksmith Forge** | East of square, near mine path. | Tool upgrades, ore refining. |

| **Carpenter's Workshop** | West edge, near forest entrance. | Farm structure upgrades. |
| **Clinic**        | Near inn, central location for emergencies. | Heals player, unlocks stamina potions. |
| **Merchant Caravan Lot** | South gate, near dock route. | Exotic trade goods, seasonal stock. |
| **Shrine of Beginnings** | Near square, glowing at night. | Acts as fast-travel hub. |

## 🔹 Farmstead Layout (Exact Placements)

- **House (Upgradeable)**: Central lot.
- **Crop Fields**: To the east, expandable via Carpenter quests.
- **Animal Pens**: To the south, separated by fences.
- **Greenhouse**: Locked to north side until Winter Year 1 quest.
- **Tool Shed & Barn**: Northwest corner.
- **Orchard Zone**: Southwest, seasonal fruit trees.
- **Fishing Pond**: Small pond in back for early-game fishing practice.

## 🔹 Biome Layout & Travel Flow

| **Biome**         | **Entry Point & Travel Path**   | **Key Features** |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------- |
| **Meadowlands**   | West gate from farm or north of Carpenter's shop. | Herbs, bees, first shrine puzzle, wildlife. |
| **Whisper Cavern** | Hidden behind waterfall in Meadowlands. | Multi-level dungeon with mine cart shortcuts. |
| **Frostpeak Highlands** | Rope bridge from Cavern Level 3. | Frozen fishing, rare ores, mountain goats. |
| **Sunset Shores** | East path from Inn, down a cliff road. | Lighthouse, docks, tropical crops. |
| **Moonlit Ruins** | Unlocked after Year 2 shrine quest; deep north forest portal. | Magical enemies, final dungeon. |

## 🔹 Fast Travel Network

- **Shrines Double as Waypoints**: Unlocks after "Shrine Awakening" quests.
- **Mine Carts**: Connect mining levels for quick traversal.
- **Boat Travel**: Optional fast travel from docks (post-Year 1 Summer).

## 🔹 Artist Implementation Details

- Map grid is **chunk-based (32x32 tiles)** per screen.
- Transition zones use **natural boundaries**: cliffs, rivers, trees.
- Seasonal map changes: **dynamic textures** for trees, ground, skybox.
- Landmarks: Lighthouse beam, shrine glow, seasonal decorations.
- Lighting progression: Warmer near town, cooler/mystical near ruins.
