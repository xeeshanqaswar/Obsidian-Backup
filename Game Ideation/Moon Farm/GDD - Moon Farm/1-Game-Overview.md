
## 1.1. Game Title

**Moon** -- A farming and life simulation RPG set in a vibrant, stylized 3D world where players cultivate their land, form relationships, and uncover the mysteries of a living, breathing world.

## 1.2. Genre

- Farming and Life Simulation
- Role-Playing Game (RPG) Elements
- Adventure and Exploration
- Narrative-Driven Sandbox

## 1.3. Target Platforms

- Nintendo Switch
- iOS / Android Mobile
- Future Potential PC Release

## 1.4. Target Audience

- Age Range: 10+ (Family-friendly with strategic depth for older audiences)
- Demographics:
  - Casual players seeking cozy, stress-free gameplay
  - Simulation and RPG fans
  - Mobile and handheld console gamers
- Accessibility: Designed for both short and long play sessions

## 1.5. Monetization Strategy

- **Nintendo Switch:** Premium one-time purchase ($29.99–$39.99)
- **Mobile:** Free-to-play with cosmetic-only in-app purchases (outfits, decorative skins). No pay-to-win or energy systems.
- **Expansion DLCs:** Future seasonal expansions (paid).

## 1.6. Unique Selling Points

1.  Fully realized **living world** with dynamic NPC schedules, festivals, and events.
2.  **Deep farming and economy simulation** with strategic crop rotation and animal breeding.
3.  **Narrative-driven progression**: Unravel mysteries of the land through exploration and quests.
4.  **Rich customization**: Player appearance, home, farm layout, tools, and outfits.
5.  Designed for **mobile-first and console-first play**, with smooth touch and controller support.

## 1.7. Development Scope and Team Setup

- **Team Size:**
  - 1 Developer (Core systems, gameplay, backend)
  - 1 Artist (Concept art, 3D modeling, UI)
- **Development Cycle:** 18–24 months (solo-dev + contracted work)
- **Tools:**
  - Engine: Unity 3D
  - 3D Art: Blender, Substance Painter
  - Audio: FMOD Integration
  - Project Management: Trello/Notion

## 1.8. Art & Visual Direction Summary

- Stylized 3D with soft lighting and vibrant colors.
- Characters are **chibi-proportioned** with expressive animations.
- Environments emphasize **coziness**: rolling hills, quaint village, lush crops, lively forests.
- Seasonal variations are visually distinct (spring blossoms, summer vibrance, autumn tones, winter snow).

## 1.9. Sound & Music Direction Summary

- Dynamic background music based on weather, time of day, and location.
- Environmental ambience: chirping birds, flowing water, wind rustle.
- Character emotes with light voice sounds (non-voiced dialog).
- Seasonal tracks to reinforce the cozy and magical tone.
