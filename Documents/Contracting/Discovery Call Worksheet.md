## 5. Fit for me

- [ ] Can I actually deliver this in my available hours?
- [ ] Does it conflict with my day job's scope or IP terms?
- [ ] Do I want to work with this person?
- [ ] Does the budget support my target effective rate?

---

## Red flags — tick any that appear

- [ ] Won't name any budget range at all, even bracketed
- [ ] Decision-maker isn't on the call and won't be named
- [ ] "We'll figure out the scope as we go"
- [ ] Wants unpaid test work or a free prototype
- [ ] Extreme urgency but resistant to a deposit
- [ ] Already burned through two or more vendors, and it's always their fault
- [ ] Asking for source access to their live game (you don't need it)

Two or more ticks: raise the price or decline.

---

## Closing (say this)

1. **Recap:** "Let me make sure I've got this — you need ____, under ____, running on ____, live before ____. Is that right?"
    
2. **Fit:** "This is squarely the kind of thing I do." _or_ "Honestly, I'm not the best fit for this — you probably want someone who ____."
    
3. **Commit:** "I'll send you a written scope with fixed price, timeline, and payment terms by ______ your time."
    
4. **Last catch:** "Anything else that might change the shape of this that we haven't covered?"
    

**Do not quote a price on the call.**
