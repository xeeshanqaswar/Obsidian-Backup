
# Shortlist (with mobile twist)

## Pushmo / Crashmo → **“Blockshift” (touch-first puzzle builder)**
- **Why it works:** Pure touch logic puzzles with short sessions; infinite design space.
- **Modern twist:** Daily challenges, creator tools, trending/community curation, “assist” hints.
- **Monetization/retention:** Free core + ad-supported hints; season passes of themed level packs; UGC creator cosmetics; battle-pass style “solve streaks”.
- **Scope (solo-ready):** ★★★★☆ — Tight core loop, scalable content via UGC.

## BOXBOY! → **“ShadowCrate” (minimalist puzzle-platformer)**

- **Why it works:** One-thumb friendly; elegant mechanics; easy to add worlds.
- **Twist:** Roguelite remix mode with shuffled rooms & mutators (low gravity, time trial).
- **Monetization:** Free first world; premium worlds; cosmetics for the box (trails/skins); optional ads on fail/retry.
- **Scope:** ★★★★☆ — Simple art, heavy on level design.

## Picross 3D Round 2 → **“VoxelCross” (3D nonogram)**

- **Why it works:** Picross has proven mobile demand; 3D is underserved.
- **Twist:** Creator mode + weekly championships; AR reveal of finished models.
- **Monetization:** Puzzle packs, VIP (no ads + early access), limited-time collab themes.
- **Scope:** ★★★★☆ — Tech for 3D carving + lots of puzzles; UGC reduces content burden.

##  Pocket Card Jockey → **“Derby Solitaire” (card + racer sim)**

- **Why it works:** Arcade solitaire + meta-progression; snackable yet sticky.
- **Twist:** Clubs & async leagues; breed/trade “bloodlines” with perks.
- **Monetization:** Battle pass, horse/cosmetic stables, stamina (light), ad-for-boosts; premium “founder horse” bundles.
- **Scope:** ★★★☆☆ — Needs tight feel + meta depth; very doable.

## Fantasy Life → **“Moonhaven” (cozy life-sim RPG)**

- **Why it works:** Life jobs + light combat + crafting—huge mobile appetite.
- **Twist:** Seasonal “jobs” (Chef Fest, Miner Derby), small co-op hubs, comfy narrative arcs.
- **Monetization:** Cosmetics housing/skins, season pass, resource accelerators (strictly fair), story DLC.
- **Scope:** ★★☆☆☆ — Bigger build; narrow the feature set to 2–3 jobs at launch.

## Style Savvy (Style Boutique) → **“Boutique Beats” (fashion shop sim)**

- **Why it works:** Fashion + storefront puzzle (supply/demand) crushes on mobile; underserved with _quality_.
- **Twist:** TikTok-like lookbook challenges; AI-driven “trends of the week”.
- **Monetization:** Cosmetic packs, collabs, VIP club, gachaless premium capsules for fairness.
- **Scope:** ★★★☆☆ — Content-hungry but modular; strong ROI if art pipeline is lean.

## Etrian Odyssey → **“AutoMap Dungeon” (touch map crawler)**

- **Why it works:** Map-drawing is perfect on touch; turn-based fits battery & sessions.
- **Twist:** Auto-mapper with manual “optimizations” for bonus loot; daily labyrinth seeds.
- **Monetization:** Expansion labyrinths, class skins/VFX, QoL VIP, no pay-to-win stats.
- **Scope:** ★★★☆☆ — Systems design > art; stylized tiles keep scope sane.

## Rhythm Heaven Megamix → **“Tap Parade” (microgame rhythm)**

- **Why it works:** Ultra-quick loops, perfect for commute play.
- **Twist:** User-mix playlists; endless score chase with weekly song modifiers.
- **Monetization:** Song packs (licensed or original), cosmetic trails/avatars; ad-supported free tier.
- **Scope:** ★★★☆☆ — Needs great feel/audio pipeline; start with 8–10 songs.

## Title (working): Nonograms Museum
- **Genre:** Puzzle / Logic (Nonograms / Picross-style)
- **Platform:** Android + iOS (Unity, 2D URP)
- **Core Loop:** Solve picture logic puzzles → reveal pixel-art image → restore sections of a virtual “museum gallery” → unlock new wings/themes.
- **Session Length:** 5–15 minutes per puzzle.
- **Target Market:** Puzzle lovers, casual gamers, cozy/relaxation players.
- **Revenue Goal:** $2,000/month stable (mix of ads + IAP packs).
