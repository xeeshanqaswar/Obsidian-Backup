# Mindset Required for Solo Development

### 1. Consistency Over Motivation

Progress comes from **daily small improvements**, not bursts of
inspiration.

Rule: \> Work even when you don't feel like it.

### 2. Problem‑Solving Mentality

Expect constant obstacles.

Mindset: \> Bugs and failures are normal parts of development.

Great developers debug patiently instead of getting frustrated.

### 3. Scope Discipline

Avoid building overly large projects.

Principle: \> Small game finished \> Big game unfinished.

### 4. Iterative Thinking

Build systems in stages.

Workflow: Prototype → Working Version → Improve → Polish

Do not try to perfect things early.

### 5. Comfort With Imperfection

Solo developers must ship things that are **good enough**, not perfect.

Perfectionism slows progress.

### 6. Curiosity & Self‑Learning

You will constantly learn new things: - tools - techniques - debugging -
optimization

Mindset: \> "I can figure this out."

### 7. Long‑Term Patience

Most solo games take **2--3 years**.

Success comes from **consistent effort over long periods**.
