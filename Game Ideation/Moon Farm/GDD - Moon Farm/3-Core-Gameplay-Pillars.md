
This section defines the **foundational mechanics** of the game. Each pillar is a major gameplay loop designed for **depth and replayability**, balancing cozy play with strategic systems.

## 3.1. Farming and Crops

- **Core Mechanic:** Players cultivate farmland by clearing debris, tilling soil, planting seeds, watering, fertilizing, and harvesting crops.
- **Depth Systems:**
  1. Crop quality (Normal, Silver, Gold, Moon-Blessed).
  2. Soil health mechanics; over-farming reduces fertility, encouraging crop rotation.
  3. Fertilizers and magical growth enhancers.
  4. Rare crops tied to moon cycles and seasonal events.
  5. Greenhouse for all-season farming (late-game).
- **Loop:**
  1. Prepare soil (Hoe, fertilizer).
  2. Plant seeds.
  3. Water daily (upgrade tools or irrigation).
  4. Harvest and sell or craft.
  5. Reinvest in farm upgrades.
- **Goal:** Create a sense of progression, ownership, and optimization as players scale farm operations.

## 3.2. Animal Husbandry

- **Core Mechanic:** Raise, breed, and care for animals that produce resources.
- **Animal Categories:**
  1. Basic livestock (chickens, cows, sheep, goats).
  2. Specialty animals (alpacas, rabbits, ducks).
  3. Magical animals (Moon Deer, Star Chickens) unlockable later.
- **Systems:**
  1. Happiness levels affect product quality.
  2. Breeding system with genetics for rare color variants.
  3. Barn/coop upgrades allow more animals and automated feeding.
  4. Seasonal care challenges (keep animals warm in winter).
- **Loop:**
  1. Feed animals and clean barns.
  2. Collect products (milk, eggs, wool).
  3. Sell or craft into goods.
  4. Upgrade animal housing.
- **Goal:** Build attachment through daily care while adding economic depth.

## 3.3. Resource Gathering

- **Core Mechanic:** Collect raw materials from the environment for crafting, construction, and upgrades.
- **Resources:**
  - Wood, stone, clay, ores, gems.
  - Wild herbs, mushrooms, and forageable items.
  - Special lunar materials from mystical biomes.
- **Loop:** Exploration and harvesting drive crafting and progression.
- **Balancing:** Resources respawn on a daily/seasonal cycle to encourage revisiting biomes.

## 3.4. Crafting and Cooking

- **Crafting:**
  - Players craft farm structures, furniture, tools, and decorations.
  - Recipes unlock through progression, friendships, and exploration.
- **Cooking:**
  - Cook meals with buffs (stamina, skill bonuses).
  - Rare recipes tied to festivals and NPC quests.
  - Players can run a small kitchen or sell crafted dishes for profit.

## 3.5. Exploration

- **Design Goal:** Encourage curiosity and gradual map discovery.
- **Mechanics:**
  - Hidden paths, locked gates, and seasonal changes alter areas.
  - Certain areas only accessible after tool upgrades (e.g., iron axe for blocked paths).
  - Secrets: treasure chests, lore tablets, rare seeds, and creatures.

## 3.6. Relationship Building (NPCs)

- **Core Mechanic:** Build bonds with villagers, unlocking stories, gifts, events, and potential romance.
- **System Depth:**
  - Affection system: Conversations, gifts, and help increase affinity.
  - Dynamic dialog: NPCs react to seasons, weather, and player choices.
  - Unlock personal storylines, cutscenes, and items through friendship milestones.
- **Romance:** Optional romantic relationships with select NPCs, leading to marriage and family.

## 3.7. Town Development & Events

- **Town Growth:**
  - Players invest in local businesses and infrastructure.
  - Upgraded town unlocks better shops, new NPCs, and festival grounds.
- **Events:**
  - Seasonal festivals, contests, and mini-games.
  - Player contributions shape town decorations and expansions.

## 3.8. Story/Quest Integration

- **Narrative Design:** Story beats are integrated naturally into daily life.
- **Quest Types:**
  - Story quests (linear progression).
  - Side quests (NPC stories, crafting requests).
  - Bulletin board tasks (daily, randomized for replayability).
  - Seasonal quests (event-driven).

## 3.9. Player Progression Systems

- **Skill Trees:**
  - Farming: Crop quality, faster watering, larger harvests.
  - Mining: Better yield, ore detection.
  - Animal Care: Happier animals, rare product bonuses.
  - Crafting: Unlock rare recipes, reduce resource costs.
  - Fishing: Easier catches, rare fish.
- **Leveling Curve:** Slow and rewarding; every level unlocks something meaningful.
- **Player Upgrades:** Tools, backpack, clothing, home expansions.
- **Farm Expansions:** New plots, buildings, and decorative options.
