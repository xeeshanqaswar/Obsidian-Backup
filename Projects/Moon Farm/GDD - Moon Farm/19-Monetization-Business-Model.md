
## 🔹 Monetization Philosophy

- **Fair, Player-First:** No predatory practices. Players should enjoy full progression without spending extra.
- **Single-Purchase on Switch/PC:** Premium pricing model, no microtransactions.
- **Mobile Version:** Free-to-start with **optional comfort & cosmetic purchases** to keep accessibility wide.
- **Replay Value through Content Updates:** Encourage engagement via seasonal events, DLC, expansions---not consumable microtransactions.

## 🔹 Pricing Strategy

| **Platform**      | **Model**              | **Notes** |
| ----------------- | ---------------------- | --------- |
| **Nintendo Switch** | $29.99 Premium        | Full experience, offline play, free updates. |
| **PC (Steam)**    | $29.99 Premium        | Mod-friendly (optional) in long-term updates. |
| **Mobile (iOS/Android)** | Free-to-start, unlock full game for $9.99 | Full game unlock; no paywalls. Cosmetic-only shop optional. |

## 🔹 Mobile Cosmetic Store

- **Content Types:**
  - Seasonal farm decoration packs (lantern sets, festive fences).
  - Non-gameplay pet skins.
  - Farm aesthetic bundles (new house styles).
- **No power progression:** Cosmetics never affect gameplay stats.
- **Pricing Range:** $0.99–$4.99 per item/pack.

## 🔹 Expansion & DLC Roadmap
