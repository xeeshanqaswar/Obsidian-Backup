##### BIOS Adjustments

- **Set Power Limits:** Change PL1 (Base Power) and PL2 (Boost Power) in your BIOS to lower values, such as capping them at 35W or 45W instead of the default 65W/117W. [[1](https://www.techreviewer.com/best-tech/intel-12500-power-supply/)]
- **Enable C-States:** Turn on deep CPU sleep states (C6/C7/C10) so the processor drops power usage to a minimum when idle.
- **Undervolt (Adaptive/Offset):** Lower the CPU core voltage slightly using an offset mode (e.g., -50mV to -100mV) to reduce heat and power draw without losing stability

### Power supply

If you are building a custom low-power server, look for a premium, lower-wattage PSU (like a 400W–500W 80-Plus Gold or Platinum unit) or an external 12V DC PicoPSU
