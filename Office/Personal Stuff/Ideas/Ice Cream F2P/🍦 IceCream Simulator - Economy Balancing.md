
## 1️⃣ Core Economy Philosophy

This economy is built around:

- 🎯 Short satisfying sessions (5–10 mins)
- 📈 Steady unlock progression (not overwhelming)
- 💰 Visible upgrade impact
- 🎁 Optional monetization (not paywall-driven)
- 🔁 Increasing order complexity over time

---

## 2️⃣ Core Currencies

| Currency      | Earned From         | Used For             | Notes              |
|---------------|---------------------|----------------------|--------------------|
| Coins         | Orders              | Equipment upgrades   | Main progression   |
| Tips          | Speed + Precision   | Cosmetic unlocks     | Skill reward       |
| Premium Gems  | IAP / Ads           | Boosts / Rare decor  | Soft monetization  |

### 3.2 Final Order Formula

```text
Final Coins =
(Container Base)
+ (Scoops × Flavor Value)
+ (Toppings Total)
+ (Sauce Value)
× Speed Bonus
```

---

### 3.3 Speed Bonus Multiplier

| Serve Time         | Multiplier |
|-------------------|------------|
| Perfect (≤80%)     | 1.4x       |
| Good               | 1.2x       |
| Slow               | 1.0x       |
| Very Slow          | 0.8x       |
