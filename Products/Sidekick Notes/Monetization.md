### Free vs. paid split

The principle: the **free tier owns "capture and organize"** so adoption isn't throttled, and the **paid tier owns "scale, sync, and the deliverable"** — because the deliverable is the part they're literally paid to produce, so that's where willingness to pay concentrates.

**Free (generous, drives installs and reviews):**

- Unlimited notes and highlight-clipping — never cap the core capture action, or people bounce before they feel the value
- Floating pinned notes, colors, pin/archive/duplicate, search
- Screenshots, but capped (e.g. ~20 stored, or a total storage limit) — this also happens to match a real technical constraint, since screenshots are heavy in `chrome.storage.local`
- Up to ~3 collections/projects — enough to try it on one real teardown, not enough for someone running many client projects
- Local storage only

**Pro (~$8–12/month, or a lifetime deal ~$39–59):**

- Unlimited collections/projects and unlimited screenshots/storage
- **Export a collection** to PDF / Markdown / CSV — this is the single most important paid feature for this audience; it turns their captured mess into the deliverable
- Cloud sync across devices
- Auto-captured source metadata on every note (URL, page title, timestamp, favicon) — so every observation is traceable and citable in an audit
- Tags + cross-project search (beyond collections)

**Team (higher tier, later):** shared collections, a read-only share link to send a client/stakeholder, comments.

Put the upgrade prompt at the moment they hit "export" or try to create a 4th project — i.e. right after they've done the work and want the payoff, never at install.
