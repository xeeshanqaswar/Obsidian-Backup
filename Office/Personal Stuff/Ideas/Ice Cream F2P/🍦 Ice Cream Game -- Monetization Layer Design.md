
## 🎯 Goal

Build a steady and sustainable monetization structure targeting
**\$3,000/month** without aggressive or predatory tactics.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1️⃣ Monetization Philosophy

-   Ads = Base income
-   Small IAP = Stability layer
-   Cosmetics = Long-term growth
-   No pay-to-win
-   No pressure selling

Monetization should feel: **Helpful, optional, aesthetic --- not
forced.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2️⃣ Revenue Structure Target

  Revenue Source    Target %
  ----------------- ----------
  Rewarded Ads      55--65%
  Small IAP Packs   20--30%
  Limited Events    10--15%

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3️⃣ Rewarded Ads System (Core Engine)

### Placement #1 -- Double Tips

After session end: \> "Watch to double today's tips?"

### Placement #2 -- Free Rare Ingredient Trial

Before session: \> "Try Premium Flavor free today?"

### Placement #3 -- Instant Upgrade Finish

> "Finish now by watching ad."

### Avoid

-   Forced interstitial spam
-   Ads during active assembly

### Expected Base Revenue Example

-   800 DAU
-   1.5 ads/day
-   \$8 eCPM

Estimated monthly ad revenue: \$300--\$450 (scales with DAU)

# 5️⃣ Soft Conversion Timing

Trigger offers when: - Player unlocks 3rd flavor - Player achieves
perfect streak - Seasonal event begins

Timing \> Frequency.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6️⃣ Cosmetic Monetization Strength

Examples: - Animated drizzle colors - Glitter sprinkles - Golden scoop
tool - Decorative containers

Cosmetics increase ARPDAU without affecting balance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7️⃣ Optional VIP Subscription (Light Model)

\$3.99/month: - +10% tips - Weekly premium rotation - Exclusive decor
badge

Even 100 subscribers = \$399/month stable income.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8️⃣ Psychological Hooks That Work

Use: - "Limited Time Flavor" - "Weekend Double Coins" - "Complete the
Dessert Set"

Avoid: - Energy systems - Hard content locks - Paywalls

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9️⃣ Realistic Revenue Scenario

If: - 1,000 DAU - ARPDAU \$0.18

Then: \$180/day → \$5,400/month gross

After platform cuts and fluctuation: ≈ \$3,000/month net achievable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🔟 Long-Term Scaling Strategy

After 4--6 months: - Add milkshakes - Add dessert cakes - Add festival
events - Add premium animated skins

Gradually increase ARPDAU from \$0.15 → \$0.25.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# ✅ Final Summary

To reach steady \$3K/month:

-   800--1000 DAU
-   1--2 rewarded ads per session
-   2--3% paying users
-   Monthly seasonal content

Keep it simple. Controlled. Sustainable.
