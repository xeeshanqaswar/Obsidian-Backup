# 🧭 Mindset for Solo Development

## 1️⃣ Consistency Over Motivation

> Progress comes from **small daily improvements**, not bursts of
> inspiration.

✔ Work even when motivation is low\
✔ Focus on steady progress

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2️⃣ Problem‑Solving Mentality

Solo development is mostly debugging.

> Bugs and failures are normal parts of development.

Successful developers remain **calm and systematic** while solving
problems.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3️⃣ Scope Discipline

Most indie projects fail because of **scope creep**.

### Golden Rule

> Small finished game \> Large unfinished game

Cut features aggressively.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4️⃣ Iterative Thinking

Build things in stages.

### Development Loop

    Prototype → Working Version → Improve → Polish

Avoid trying to make the first version perfect.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5️⃣ Comfort With Imperfection

Perfectionism kills projects.

Solo developers must ship things that are **good enough**, not perfect.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6️⃣ Curiosity & Self‑Learning

Solo developers constantly learn:

-   tools
-   debugging
-   optimization
-   new workflows

### Mindset

> "I don't know yet, but I can figure it out."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7️⃣ Long‑Term Patience

Most solo games take **2--3 years**.

Success comes from **consistent effort over long periods of time**.
