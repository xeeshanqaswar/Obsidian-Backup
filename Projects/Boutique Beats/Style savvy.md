## ⚙️ How Indies Use It Strategically

As a solo dev, a vertical slice is your **“Mini Masterpiece”** — it’s how you:

1. Learn what’s really involved in finishing a game.
    
2. Impress players/publishers with a real feel of your vision.
    
3. Avoid feature creep before you’ve even shipped something.
    

You don’t have to make a vertical slice _first_ — you usually reach it after MVP → greybox → vertical slice.

## 📈 Production Flow Example (Simplified)

1. **Prototype / MVP (2–3 weeks)**  
    → “Is it fun?”
    
2. **Greybox Production (2–3 months)**  
    → “Are the systems working together?”
    
3. **Vertical Slice (1–2 months)**  
    → “Is this what we’ll ship?”
    
4. **Full Game Production (rest of dev time)**  
    → “Expand content and polish based on slice.”
